EmpathIQ — Project State Summary
What it is
An emotionally intelligent AI companion — React+Vite frontend (artifacts/empathiq), Express+TypeScript API (artifacts/api-server), Drizzle ORM + PostgreSQL. Three right-panel tabs: Chat, Voice (Hume AI), Smart Glasses.
