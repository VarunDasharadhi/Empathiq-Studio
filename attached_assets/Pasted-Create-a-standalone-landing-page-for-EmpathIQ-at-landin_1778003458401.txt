Create a standalone landing page for EmpathIQ at /landing.html (or a separate route /landing). This page is for investors, sponsors, and buildathon judges. The goal is to make them feel the product before they even click the live app link. It should feel alive, emotional, and unlike anything they've seen from an AI product.
Aesthetic direction:
Dark, cinematic, premium. Think neuroscience meets human warmth. Background is near black with a subtle animated noise texture and deep teal and violet gradient glow that pulses slowly like a heartbeat. Typography uses a sharp editorial display font for headlines (try DM Serif Display or Playfair Display from Google Fonts) paired with a clean modern body font (try DM Sans). No generic purple on white AI startup clichés. This should feel closer to an Apple keynote slide than a hackathon project.
Page structure and content:
Section 1, Hero:
Full viewport height. Animated headline that types out: "It doesn't just hear you. It sees you." Subheading below: "EmpathIQ reads your face, your voice, and the gap between what you say and how you actually feel, in real time." Below that, two CTA buttons side by side: "Try the live app" linking to https://empathiq-studio--varundasharadhi.replit.app and "Watch demo" linking to https://www.loom.com/share/ee3177d34b40404487115fca5f8366ed. Add a slow looping animated gradient orb behind the text. In the bottom corner, a subtle pulsing emotion chip UI element showing fake live readings (Happy 72%, Anxious 18%) to hint at what the product does.
Section 2, The Problem:
Bold left-aligned statement: "Most AI listens. None of them actually notice." Three short pain points laid out horizontally: "Chatbots respond to words, not feelings.", "Therapy is expensive and inaccessible.", "There is no tool that reads the room." Each has a simple icon and two lines of text. Dark card style with a faint border glow.
Section 3, How It Works:
Heading: "Three signals. One emotional truth." Three steps shown as a horizontal flow with connecting lines: Step 1 "Face" with a webcam icon, reads micro expressions in real time using face-api.js. Step 2 "Voice" with a waveform icon, detects tone and emotional texture via Hume EVI. Step 3 "Words" with a chat icon, Claude API responds with genuine empathy not scripted advice. Below the flow, one sentence: "When your face says sad but your words say fine, EmpathIQ notices."
Section 4, Features:
Heading: "Built in 24 hours. Feels like it took years." Mosaic card grid showing 6 features: Live emotion detection with confidence scoring, 8 AI personality modes from Therapist to Anger Release, Smart Glasses tab for reading others' emotions, Emotional Arc session history, Hume EVI expressive voice mode, Privacy mode and voice toggle. Each card has a relevant emoji, short title, and one line description. Cards have a glassmorphism style with subtle hover glow animation.
Section 5, The Vision:
Full width dark section with a large centred quote: "The AI that speaks for those who can't." Below it: "Built for non-verbal children, elderly care, mental health access, and anyone who has ever felt misunderstood." Then a horizontal list of future directions: Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses integration, Apple Watch biometric fusion, Guardian Mode for carers, Clinical HIPAA compliant version. Each with a small icon.
Section 6, Built By:
Simple centred section. "Built solo in 24 hours for the Replit 10 Buildathon." Show the tech stack as small badge chips: face-api.js, Claude API, Hume EVI, Replit. Add the Buildathon project link as a subtle text link.
Section 7, CTA footer:
Large centred heading: "Ready to feel the difference?" Two buttons: "Try EmpathIQ live" and "View on Buildathon". Below: small text with GitHub link and the tagline "Empathy, engineered."
Animations and interactions:
Staggered fade-up reveal as each section enters the viewport using IntersectionObserver. The hero emotion chip cycles through different emotion readings every 3 seconds with a smooth transition. The hero headline types out on load. The gradient orb in the hero pulses slowly on a loop. Card hover states lift slightly with a glow border. All transitions use ease-in-out at 300ms.
Technical requirements:
Single HTML file with embedded CSS and JS, no frameworks needed. Google Fonts for typography. No external JS libraries except what is already in the Replit project. Mobile responsive with a stacked single column layout under 768px. On mobile, the three-step flow stacks vertically and the feature grid becomes two columns. Both CTA buttons stack vertically on mobile.
Do not use Inter, Roboto, or Arial. Do not use purple on white. Do not make it look like a generic AI product landing page. Make it feel like something a VC would screenshot and share.
